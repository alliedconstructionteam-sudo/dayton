Who we are

A.C.T. is here for the long run

Allied Construction Team (A.C.T.) is a residential Lawn & Grounds company serving Dayton, Ohio. Our work includes mowing, trimming, recurring lawn maintenance, and seasonal leaf cleanup.

A.C.T. began as a student-led effort to learn, build practical skills, and serve the community. That beginning remains part of our origin story as we grow into a lasting company focused on outdoor property care for local homeowners.

We are building for the long term through clear expectations, careful work, and communication shaped around each property.
