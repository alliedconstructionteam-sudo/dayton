Who We Are

About A.C.T.

Allied Construction Team (A.C.T.) is a residential Lawn & Grounds company serving Dayton, Ohio. Our services include mowing, trimming, recurring lawn maintenance, and seasonal leaf cleanup, with clear expectations before work begins.

A.C.T. began as a student-led effort to learn, build practical skills, and serve the community. That origin remains part of our story as we grow into a long-term company focused on dependable outdoor property care for local homeowners.

We are building for the long term through careful work, straightforward communication, and services shaped around each property.
